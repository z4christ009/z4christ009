A7AA Barber Shop started the way most good things around here start — with a chair on the rooftop, a mirror, and friends who needed a cut.

Two years and a lot of practice later, that same chair is booked most days of the week. Every fade gets blended until it disappears, every line gets checked twice, and nobody leaves before they've seen the back of their own head in the mirror.
